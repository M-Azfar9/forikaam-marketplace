According to Pakistan's Labour Force Survey 2024-25, 80.8% of the country's employed workforce operates in the informal economy, working as electricians, plumbers, mechanics, welders, and construction workers. Yet hiring one of them remains a frustrating, opaque, and often unsafe experience for Pakistani households and businesses.

ForiKaam is a mobile-first reverse-auction labor marketplace purpose-built for Pakistan. Think of it as inDrive, but for the informal labor sector. Homeowners post a parameterized job request and name their price. Verified, NADRA-authenticated workers in the area bid in real time. Both parties negotiate transparently and strike a deal. No middlemen, no fixed markup, no information asymmetry. The culture of bargaining (molbhav), digitized, systematized, and made safe.

# **SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM**

## **What Problem Does ForiKaam Solve?**

Approximately 80.8% of Pakistan's employed workforce operates in the informal sector as per the Labour Force Survey 2024-25, which includes construction workers, electricians, plumbers, mechanics, welders, and carpenters who are completely invisible to the newly rising digital economy. The problem is not that workers don't exist. The problem is a four-headed market failure.

**1\. Trust Deficit**

You can't safely invite any stranger into your home with zero background checks or a trust reference from a reliable source. This isn't just a mere concern — it is the single most cited reason middle-class Pakistani households, especially those with women at home, have trouble hiring from the informal market.

**2\. Discovery Inefficiency**

In 2026, people still physically walk to a chowk or rely on word-of-mouth to find a labor worker. While millennials and older generations have built an 'ustaad' network for every kind of work in their phonebook over decades, the rapidly growing younger population lacks these connections and is more vulnerable to scams, fraud, or poor-quality work. Pakistan's population is 68% under 30 — this demographic has no inherited rolodex and no trusted alternative.

**3\. Pricing Ambiguity**

Neither the homeowner nor the worker knows what a fair price is, and both get exploited. The homeowner overpays out of desperation or inexperience; the worker underpays because middlemen (thekedar) extract 30-50% of their wage just for making the introduction.

**4\. Skill Invisibility**

A 15-year experienced electrician and a day-laborer who just picked up a screwdriver last week stand at the same chowk, competing on the same terms. No credentials, no rating, no public record of past work or conduct. As a result, price becomes the only signal — driving quality out of the market entirely.

**Scale of the Problem**

According to HIES 2024-25, urban households spend around 26% of income on housing and utilities, and a further 3.25% on furnishing and household maintenance, yet there is no reliable digital channel to hire the people who service those needs. The global market for online on-demand home services is growing at 80.3% CAGR. Pakistan is at the very beginning of this curve. Despite the scale of this market, Pakistan still lacks a trusted digital infrastructure connecting households to verified service professionals.

# **SECTION 2: THE SOLUTION — ForiKaam**

## **What Is ForiKaam?**

ForiKaam is a mobile marketplace that connects Pakistani households with verified, skilled labor workers through a structured, real-time bidding system, where the homeowner names their price, and nearby workers compete for the job. Think of it as inDrive, but for Pakistan's informal labor sector.

Pakistan's labor market doesn't need more workers. It needs a trust layer, a discovery engine, and a fair pricing mechanism, built for the way Pakistanis actually think and negotiate. Here is how ForiKaam solves each of the four problems directly.

Against the trust deficit, every worker on ForiKaam is biometrically verified against Pakistan's national ID database through NADRA's Nishan Pakistan API, in under 60 seconds, at Rs. 20 per verification, with no physical office required. A homeowner browsing the app sees only workers with a verified identity badge. They are not inviting a stranger. They are inviting someone whose identity has been confirmed by the state.

Against discovery inefficiency, a homeowner opens the app, selects a service category, and sees available verified workers near them in real time. No chowk visit, no favor-calling, no waiting until morning.

Against pricing opacity, we don't impose a fixed price. Instead, the homeowner states what they are willing to pay, and workers in the vicinity bid to accept, counter-offer, or decline. This is the exact bargaining culture Pakistanis already live by, digitized and made safe. Both parties arrive at a price they agreed to, not one an algorithm dictated. inDrive's success in Pakistan is proof that this model works.

Against skill invisibility, every completed job builds a worker's public profile: ratings, reviews, job count, and specialty badges. Over time, the 15-year veteran electrician is no longer indistinguishable from the day-laborer. Their record speaks for them, and the market rewards them accordingly.

One deliberate design decision separates ForiKaam from every failed predecessor: the job posting is not a free-text box. The homeowner is guided through a structured decision tree, service type, specific problem, estimated duration, materials needed, before any bidding begins. This eliminates the ambiguity that killed Pakistan's last bidding platform, Supertasker, where workers were forced to bid blind on vague requests, quality collapsed, and the platform lost user trust within months.

Payment is processed through JazzCash, Easypaisa, or cash, covering both the digitally banked and the entirely unbanked worker population.

The result is a marketplace where a homeowner in Lahore can post a plumbing job, receive three bids from verified workers within five minutes, pick the best one, and have someone at the door, with a government-confirmed identity and a public track record, within the hour.

## **How the Product Works — The 5-Step Flow**

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **STEP 1** | HOMEOWNER POSTS A PARAMETERIZED JOB The app guides the user through a structured decision tree (not a free-text box). Example: Plumbing → Leaking Pipe → Kitchen → Under-sink → Estimated Duration: 1 hour → Materials: Not required. This kills ambiguity before it starts. |
| **STEP 2** | AI SUGGESTS A FAIR PRICE RANGE Based on historical transaction data for that job type, locality, and time of day, the app suggests a fair market price range (e.g., Rs. 800 – Rs. 1,400). The homeowner sets their offered price within or around this band. |
| **STEP 3** | VERIFIED WORKERS RECEIVE REAL-TIME NOTIFICATIONS All NADRA-verified, ForiKaam-registered workers within a 5km geofenced radius receive a push notification with the job card. They can: Accept immediately, Counter-bid with their preferred price, or Decline. |
| **STEP 4** | HOMEOWNER SELECTS & CONFIRMS The homeowner reviews all bids, along with each worker's rating, completed job count, and verification badge. They select the best fit, and a job confirmation and worker ETA appears on both screens. |
| **STEP 5** | JOB COMPLETION, ESCROW RELEASE & RATING Payment (cash or in-app wallet) is confirmed upon completion. Both parties rate each other. The platform takes a 10% commission. The worker's reputation score grows with every successful job. |

## **The Trust Infrastructure — NADRA Integration**

ForiKaam's most important differentiator is the mandatory use of NADRA's 'Nishan Pakistan' biometric verification API for every worker on the platform. This is not optional or delayed — it is the very first step of worker registration.

Here is exactly how it works: The worker downloads the ForiKaam Karigar app. They photograph their CNIC (National ID Card) and capture a live selfie. The app calls the NADRA Nishan Pakistan API in real-time, which biometrically matches the live selfie against the national CNIC database. The entire process costs approximately Rs. 20 per verification and takes under 60 seconds. No physical office. No manual review. No fraud.

Workers who pass verification receive a prominent 'ForiKaam Verified' badge — a blue shield icon visible on every job card and profile. This badge is the platform's core promise to homeowners: every worker in this app has been identity-verified against Pakistan's national database.

**The Three Moats**

**The first advantage is architectural, not operational: parameterized job posting.**

Every previous attempt at a bidding-based labor platform in Pakistan collapsed for the same reason. When homeowners post vague, free-text requests like 'need a plumber,' workers are forced to bid blind. The lowest bidder wins, quality collapses, and both sides stop trusting the platform. Pakistan has already lived through this with Supertasker, by far the most similar startup we identified, which attracted thousands of users but failed to retain them because the open-ended job structure created chaos no moderation team could fix.

Unlike Supertasker, which targeted white-collar freelance tasks, ForiKaam is built specifically for Pakistan's informal blue-collar labor sector, a structurally different supply side with different verification needs, different UI requirements, and a completely different trust problem.

ForiKaam eliminates the ambiguity at the architecture level. Before any bid is placed, the homeowner completes a structured decision tree: service type, specific problem, estimated duration, materials required. The bid happens around a fully specified job, not a vague description. This is not a feature. It is the core design decision that makes a bidding model viable for physical labor, and no competitor in Pakistan has built it.

**The second is a timing advantage with genuine defensibility: NADRA's Nishan Pakistan API.**

In February 2026, NADRA launched Nishan Pakistan, a unified platform giving regulated private sector organizations access to multi-biometric verification APIs, including fingerprint, facial recognition, and demographic verification. NADRA's chairman described it as specifically designed to empower startups with secure, contactless biometric verification.

ForiKaam integrates this from day one. Every worker is verified against Pakistan's national ID database before they appear in a single search result, at Rs. 20 per worker, in under 60 seconds, with no physical office or manual review required. The trust layer that has taken competitors weeks to build per worker becomes a 60-second automated step at registration, a structural advantage that compounds as the worker base scales.

**The third is market evidence that this exact model already works in Pakistan: inDrive.**

Since launching in Pakistan in 2021, inDrive's ride volumes rose nearly 40% year over year in 2025, while courier deliveries increased 67% in the first half of the year, making Pakistan one of its fastest-growing markets globally. The driver is simple: Pakistanis actively prefer negotiating their own price over accepting one an algorithm sets. ForiKaam takes that proven behavioral preference and applies it to a market where the stakes are higher, price variance is larger, and the demand for trust is far more acute.

inDrive's super-app expansion in Pakistan covers ride-hailing, grocery delivery, and in-app advertising. Home services, the largest and most fragmented category of informal labor spending in Pakistan, remains completely uncontested by any well-funded, structured platform. That is the window ForiKaam is entering.

# **SECTION 6: TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE**

## **System Design Overview**

ForiKaam's technical architecture is a real-time, event-driven microservices system. The complexity is high — this is not a simple CRUD application. The core innovation is a live auction engine that must handle concurrent bids, race conditions, and geospatial matching at sub-300ms latency.

|     |     |     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Microservice** | **Technology** | **Purpose** | **Key Challenge** | **Solution** |
| Identity & Auth Service | Node.js + JWT + NADRA Nishan API | Worker onboarding & biometric verification | NADRA API reliability & latency | Local caching of verification status + SMS fallback |
| Geospatial Matching Engine | PostGIS / MongoDB Geo Index | Match job requests to nearby workers within 5km radius | Efficient indexing of 100k+ worker locations | Geospatial indexing + worker 'heartbeat' location updates every 2 min |
| Real-Time Bidding Engine | WebSockets + Redis Pub/Sub + DynamoDB | Manage live auction state, bids, counter-bids | Race conditions on concurrent bid acceptance | Distributed locks (Redis SETNX) + optimistic concurrency control |
| Notification Service | Firebase FCM + Jazz/Telenor SMS API | Push notifications to workers, SMS fallback | Low-end devices dropping persistent connections | Hybrid push + SMS fallback for all critical events |
| Payment Service | JazzCash API + Easypaisa API + escrow logic | Transaction processing & escrow | Mobile wallet API instability | Retry logic + dual wallet integration for redundancy |

## **Mobile App Design Principles (Worker App)**

The Karigar app design is the most critical UX challenge. The target user often has limited literacy, low-end hardware, and is working in physically demanding environments. Every design decision must serve this reality:

- **App Size < 15MB** — optimized for 2GB RAM devices running Android Go
- **Voice Readout** — all job notifications are automatically read aloud in Urdu (text-to-speech)
- **Icon-Based Job Cards** — universally recognized icons (wrench, water drop, bolt, brick) for each trade type
- **3-Button Interface** — Accept (green), Counter-Bid (orange), Decline (red) — maximum three taps for any action
- **Offline Resilience** — SMS fallback ensures workers receive job alerts even without active internet

# **CONCLUSION: THE FORIKAAM OPPORTUNITY**
